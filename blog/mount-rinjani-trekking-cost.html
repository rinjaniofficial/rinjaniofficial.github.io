What the trek-only price includes

  • Experienced English-speaking guide and porter team
  • Camping gear: tent, sleeping bag, mattress and toilet tent
  • All meals during the trek (three a day), drinks and fresh fruit
  • Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) entrance ticket
  • Transport after the trek to Bangsal Harbor, Senggigi or Mataram

What is not included

  • Pick-up from your hotel or airport before the trek
  • Hotel or homestay before and after the trek
  • Travel insurance (required — bring your own policy)
  • Personal porter for your daypack
  • Tips for guide and porters
  • Personal hiking gear

Why sharing group treks cost less

Guides, porters, cooks, tents and park logistics cost roughly the same whether two or eight people trek. In a sharing group those costs are split between up to 8 trekkers, so each person pays far less than on a private crew. You still get the same mountain, sunrise and lake — you just share them, and usually make friends.

How to compare Rinjani tour prices fairly

A very cheap price can mean cut corners. Ask every operator:

  1. Is the park entrance ticket included?
  2. What is the group size? (Ours: maximum 8 pax.)
  3. What food is provided? Three meals a day, drinks and fruit.
  4. What camping equipment is included? Tent, sleeping bag, mattress and toilet tent.
  5. How many porters and guides? And are they paid fairly?
  6. Is transport after the trek included?
  7. What is excluded? Insurance, tips and hotel nights add up.

Extra costs to budget for

  • Travel insurance that covers trekking up to about 3,700 m
  • Hotel before the trek in Senaru, Sembalun or Mataram
  • Getting to the trailhead if you choose trek only
  • Tips for your guide and porters (at your discretion)
  • Gear rental if you do not own what you need
  • Snacks and drinks before and after the trek

Ways to save on a Rinjani trek

  • Choose a sharing group instead of a private trek.
  • Pick shoulder-season dates (May, June, September).
  • Travel with friends and request the same departure.
  • Bring your own basic gear.
  • Book trek only and arrange your own transport if you are already near Senaru or Sembalun.
