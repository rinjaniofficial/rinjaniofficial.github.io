Best Time to Climb Mount Rinjani: Month-by-Month Guide

The short answer: the dry season, roughly April to October. Here is what each month really feels like on the mountain, so you can choose dates that give you clear skies, safe trails and a sunrise worth the 2 a.m. wake-up.

Quick answer: April to October is the best window

Mount Rinjani (3,726 m) has a tropical climate with a clear dry season and a wet season. For almost every trekker the dry season from about April to October is the best time to climb: trails are firmer, skies are clearer, and the summit sunrise and the view down to Segara Anak Lake are far more likely to be visible.

Best balance of weather and crowds: May, June and September. Most reliable clear skies (and busiest): July and August.

Dry season vs wet season on Rinjani

Dry season (April – October)

You get stable weather, firm footing on the savanna and forest sections, and the best odds of a clear summit morning. The trade-off is more trekkers on the main routes in July and August, and dusty, loose volcanic sand on the final summit climb.

Wet season (November – March)

Rain turns the trail slippery, rivers can rise, and clouds often hide the crater lake. Park access can be restricted, especially in the wettest months. If your travel dates force you into this period, choose a flexible booking and take good travel insurance.

When is the summit sunrise best?

Summit day starts in the middle of the night — usually around 2 or 3 a.m. from the Sembalun crater rim — so you reach the top for sunrise. The clearest sunrises happen in the dry months, especially June to September. Even then, clouds can roll in, so treat a perfect sunrise as a bonus rather than a promise. The 2D1N Sembalun Rim & Summit and 3D2N Sembalun – Senaru treks are both built around this summit push.

Which route suits which season?

  • Peak dry season (Jul – Aug): any route works. Sembalun mornings are cold and clear; Senaru’s rainforest stays pleasantly shaded.
  • Hot months (Sep – Oct): the exposed Sembalun savanna can be very warm by midday, so start early and carry plenty of water.
  • Shoulder months (Apr – Jun, Nov): the 2D1N Crater Rim Senaru trek skips the summit push, which makes it a good lower-risk option if the weather looks uncertain.

Tips for picking your dates

  1. Add buffer days. Do not schedule your trek on the day before an international flight.
  2. Book popular dates early. July and August departures with sharing groups (max 8 pax) fill up first.
  3. Check park status. Trails can close after heavy rain, earthquakes or volcanic activity. Ask us on WhatsApp for the latest.
  4. Bring travel insurance. It is required for all our treks and protects you if plans change.

Frequently asked questions

Can I climb Mount Rinjani in the rainy season?

It is possible in the shoulder months, but trails are slippery, visibility is poor and the park can restrict access, especially in the wettest months. We recommend the dry season, roughly April to October.

Which is the coldest month on Rinjani?

July and August usually have the coldest nights at the crater rim, and the summit can feel close to freezing with wind. Bring a warm jacket, hat and gloves.

Is Mount Rinjani open all year?

No. The national park is usually closed to trekkers for part of the wet season. Check current trail status with your operator before booking.

How far in advance should I book a Rinjani trek?

For July and August we recommend booking several weeks ahead. For other months a shorter lead time is usually fine. Message us on WhatsApp for current availability.
