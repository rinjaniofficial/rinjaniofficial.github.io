Terms & Conditions

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Operator: Rinjani Official, Senaru, Lombok

The terms that apply when you book a Mount Rinjani sharing group trek with Rinjani Official.

1. About these terms

These terms apply to every booking made with Rinjani Official (“we”, “us”), based at Jl. Pariwisata, Desa Senaru, Kec. Bayan, North Lombok Regency, NTB 83354, Indonesia, and to your use of this website. By booking a trek, you confirm that you have read and accepted them.

2. Sharing group treks

All our packages are sharing group (join group) treks. You trek together with other travellers on the same route and dates, in a group of up to 8 people. We do not run private treks. The group may include travellers you did not book with.

3. Booking and confirmation

  • You can enquire and book through the booking form, WhatsApp or email.
  • A booking is confirmed only after we reply to confirm availability and the details of your package, date and number of trekkers.
  • Please give us accurate names, passport details and contact information. Mistakes may delay park registration.
  • Payment method and timing are agreed with you on WhatsApp before your booking is confirmed.

4. Prices

Prices are shown in US dollars (USD) per person on our price page and in the package pages. Trek-only prices do not include pick-up or hotel before the trek, which are available in our Full Package option.

Included in trek-only prices

  • Guide and porter team
  • Camping equipment (tent, sleeping bag, mattress and toilet tent)
  • Meals during the trek, drinks and fruit
  • Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) entrance ticket
  • Transport after the trek to Bangsal Harbor, Senggigi or Mataram

Not included

  • Pick-up and hotel before and after the trek (unless you book the Full Package option)
  • Travel insurance
  • Personal porter for your bag, tips for guides and porters, and personal hiking gear
  • Flights and other travel to and from Lombok

Package details and inclusions on the website are the guide to what is included in your booking.

5. Travel insurance

Travel insurance is required. It must cover trekking up to about 3,700 m and emergency medical treatment and evacuation. You must give us your insurance provider and policy number before the trek. You are responsible for checking that your policy covers your trek, and for any cost not covered.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

  • Rescheduling: you can ask to move your trek to another date. A rescheduling fee applies, and we confirm the fee and conditions with you on WhatsApp.
  • Cancellation and refunds: the conditions that apply to your booking are confirmed in writing with you on WhatsApp before you pay.
  • Please contact us as early as possible. Changes made close to the trek date may not be possible.

7. Changes, weather and park closures

Mount Rinjani is an active volcano in a national park. Weather, trail conditions, volcanic or seismic activity, or decisions by the park authorities may require us to change a route, a schedule or a campsite, or to cancel a trek for safety. We will tell you as soon as we can and discuss options with you.

8. Fitness, health and safety

  • Trekking Rinjani is physically demanding. You confirm that you are in good health and fit enough for the package you choose.
  • Tell us before the trek about any injury, medical condition or medication that could affect you on the mountain.
  • Our guide is responsible for the safety of the group. The guide may change the pace or route, or ask any trekker to turn back, if this is needed for safety. Please follow the guide’s instructions.

9. Risks and liability

Mountain trekking carries risks, including falls, injury, altitude and weather effects, illness and, in rare cases, serious harm. By booking, you accept these risks. To the extent allowed by law, we are not liable for loss, injury or delay caused by events outside our reasonable control, by your own actions or by failure to follow instructions. Nothing in these terms limits any rights you have under law that cannot be excluded.

10. Behaviour and environment

  • Carry out all rubbish and do not leave waste on the mountain.
  • Respect other trekkers, the crew, local culture and the sacred character of the mountain and lake.
  • Do not use drugs, light fires or damage plants and wildlife.
  • We may end a trekker’s participation without refund if their behaviour endangers or seriously disturbs others.

11. Using this website

We take care that the website is accurate, but prices, availability and trail information may change. Contact us on WhatsApp to confirm before you book.
