Privacy Policy

Effective date: 31 October 2026 · Operator: Rinjani Official, Senaru, Lombok

How we collect, use and protect your personal information when you book a trek or visit our website.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Rinjani Official, a Mount Rinjani trekking operator based at Jl. Pariwisata, Desa Senaru, Kec. Bayan, North Lombok Regency, NTB 83354, Indonesia. In this policy, “we”, “us” and “our” mean Rinjani Official. You can contact us at rinjaniofficial999@gmail.com or on WhatsApp at +62 823-2305-0467.

2. Information we collect

We keep data collection to what we need to organise your trek.

  • Booking and enquiry details: your name, contact details, travel dates, number of trekkers, chosen package and meeting point, which you give us through our booking form, WhatsApp or email.
  • Documents for park registration and safety: passport or ID details and your travel insurance provider and policy number.
  • Health information you choose to share (for example an injury or medical condition) so that your guide can keep you safe.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type, device, pages viewed and approximate location, collected through cookies and similar technologies.

3. How we use your information

  • To answer your questions and confirm, organise and run your booking.
  • To register you with Gunung Rinjani National Park (TNGR) as required.
  • To arrange transport, guides and porters, and to keep you safe on the mountain.
  • To improve our website and measure how our advertising performs.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where needed:

  • Our guides, drivers and trek team, so that they can run your trek.
  • The national park authority and other officials, for registration and safety requirements.
  • Service providers such as WhatsApp (messaging), Google (fonts, analytics and advertising) and our website hosting provider.
  • Emergency services, your insurer or your emergency contact, if there is an accident or emergency.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

5. WhatsApp and booking messages

When you press a WhatsApp button or submit our booking form, your message opens in WhatsApp with the details you entered. Messages are then handled under WhatsApp’s own terms and privacy policy. Please do not send payment card numbers or other highly sensitive data in chat.

6. Cookies, analytics and advertising

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including those from Google, to remember preferences, understand how the site is used and measure and improve advertising such as Google Ads. Google may use cookies to show ads based on your previous visits to this or other websites. You can control or delete cookies in your browser settings and manage Google ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.

The site also loads fonts and icons from third-party services (Google Fonts and Font Awesome), which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as needed to organise your trek, handle questions or complaints, and meet legal and accounting requirements. After that we delete it or anonymise it.

8. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss and unauthorised access. No internet service can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please share only what is necessary.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict certain uses, and to withdraw consent. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indonesian law, including the Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). To make a request, email us at rinjaniofficial999@gmail.com.

10. Children

Our website is not aimed at children. Bookings for travellers under 18 must be made by a parent or legal guardian.

11. Links to other websites

Our site may link to third-party websites (for example Google Maps or WhatsApp). We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of the page shows when it was last changed.
