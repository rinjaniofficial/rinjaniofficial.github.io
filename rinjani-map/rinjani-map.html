Where is Mount Rinjani?

Mount Rinjani is an active volcano on the island of Lombok, in West Nusa Tenggara (NTB), Indonesia, just east of Bali. The mountain and its crater lake are protected inside Gunung Rinjani National Park. The two main trailheads are Sembalun (east) and Senaru (north); the Torean trail is a popular exit on the Senaru side.

Route breakdown

Sembalun route (east)

The trail starts at Sembalun (about 1,060 m), crosses savanna and passes three rest posts before reaching Plawangan Sembalun, the crater rim camp at about 2,640 m. Day one is roughly 9 km with a climb of about 1,700 m. The summit is reached from here in a pre-dawn climb.

Senaru route (north)

From Senaru the trail enters the national park (about 800 m), climbs through rainforest past three rest posts and reaches Plawangan Senaru, the crater rim at about 2,620 m. The climb is shorter in distance than Sembalun but steeper. From the rim you descend to Segara Anak Lake.

Segara Anak Lake and hot springs

Inside the caldera, Segara Anak (about 2,010 m) is the crater lake where many treks camp. The natural hot springs (Aiq Kalak) are a short walk away, at about 1,960 m, and are a favourite way to rest after the summit.

Torean exit

The Torean trail leaves the lake area through forest and valley to Torean village (about 600 m), usually quieter than the main descent. See our 3D2N Sembalun to Torean package.

Rinjani map FAQ

Where is Mount Rinjani?

Mount Rinjani is on the island of Lombok in West Nusa Tenggara (NTB), Indonesia, east of Bali. It is inside Gunung Rinjani National Park.

Where do the Rinjani trails start?

The two main trailheads are Sembalun in East Lombok and Senaru in North Lombok. The Torean trail is a popular exit route on the Senaru side.

How high are the crater rims?

Plawangan Sembalun is at about 2,640 m and Plawangan Senaru at about 2,620 m. The summit is 3,726 m and Segara Anak Lake is at roughly 2,010 m.

Which route does the map show for the summit?

The summit is usually reached from the Sembalun crater rim in a pre-dawn climb. Cross-route treks then descend to Segara Anak Lake and out via Senaru or Torean.
