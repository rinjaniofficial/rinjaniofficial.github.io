About Us

More Than Just a Cheap Price

Get to know Rinjani Official — and why we care as much about quality as we do about affordability.

Our Story

Rinjani Official runs Sharing Group treks up Mount Rinjani — and we know that "sharing group" often makes people think "cheap, so probably basic." That's not how we see it.

Our packages are priced to be accessible, but we don't cut corners on the things that actually matter on the mountain: proper food and safe drinking water, reliable tents, and quality camping gear. A low price means nothing if you're cold at night because the tent leaks, or exhausted because the meals weren't enough to keep you going. So while other operators compete purely on who can charge the least, we'd rather compete on making sure every climber — whether it's their first trek or their tenth — comes down safe, well-fed, and glad they chose to go up.

For a lot of our guests, climbing Mount Rinjani is a once-in-a-lifetime trip. We take that seriously, and we build every package around it.

Why Trek With Us

Official TNGR Permits

Every trek is registered through the Mount Rinjani National Park Authority (TNGR) — no shortcuts, no unofficial routes.

Licensed Guides & Experienced Porters

Our guide and porter teams know the Sembalun, Senaru, and Torean routes well, and are trained to watch for signs of altitude sickness and fatigue.

Real Meals, Not an Afterthought

Freshly cooked food three times a day, fresh fruit, and plenty of drinking water — enough to keep you going, not just enough to check a box.

Proper Tents & Camping Gear

Dome tents, sleeping bags, sleeping mats, and a private portable toilet tent at every campsite — built to handle Rinjani's cold nights.

Sharing Group, Max 8 Pax

Small enough to stay personal and safe, while keeping the price accessible by sharing guide and logistics costs.

Safety-First Requirements

We require travel insurance covering trekking activities for every climber — because being prepared matters more than being fast.
