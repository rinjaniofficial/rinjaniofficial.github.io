Requirements & Conditions

Health & Fitness Condition

  • Participants should be in good physical condition and used to long-distance walking (recommended: at least 2–4 weeks of physical training before departure)
  • Participants with a history of heart disease, severe asthma, uncontrolled hypertension, or who are pregnant are not advised to join this trek
  • Please inform our team of any medical conditions or allergies before departure
  • Bring any personal medication needed for the trip
For safety reasons, our guides reserve the right to stop a participant's climb if their physical condition makes it unsafe to continue.

Preparing Before Your Trek

30 Days Before

Start light cardio training (brisk walking, jogging, stair climbing) 3–4 times a week to build stamina.

7 Days Before

Recheck your personal gear (boots, jacket, headlamp) and make sure your boots are already broken in to avoid blisters.

1 Day Before

Get enough rest, avoid staying up late, and arrive at the meeting point for the briefing session with your guide.

Understanding Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS)

Above 3,000m, some climbers may experience AMS symptoms such as mild headache, nausea, or dizziness due to lower oxygen levels. Our guides are trained to recognize these symptoms and will adjust the walking pace to help your body adapt gradually.

Tips to reduce risk: stay hydrated by drinking water regularly, keep a steady walking pace (don't rush), and let your guide know immediately if you feel unwell.

Day One

Sembalun Village → Plawangan Sembalun

Registration at the Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) entrance in Sembalun.

Begin trekking across the open Sembalun savanna.

D-2
Day Two

Summit Push → Descend to Sembalun

02:00

Wake up, light snack and hot drink, then begin the summit push.

06:00

Arrive at the 3,726m summit right at sunrise. Enjoy panoramic views of Lombok, Sumbawa, and Bali.

07:30

Descend back to Plawangan Sembalun campsite for breakfast and pack up.

09:00

Descend from Plawangan Sembalun towards Sembalun Village.

13:00

Arrive at Sembalun Village. Free drop-off to the Bangsal, Senggigi, or Mataram area. Trek complete!

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this package suitable for beginner hikers?

This is our most physically demanding package, since the fast pace leaves less time to acclimatize before the summit push. We recommend it for participants with some prior hiking experience and a solid base fitness level.

What happens if the weather is bad during the summit push?

Safety is our top priority. If conditions are unsafe, the guide may delay or adjust the summit attempt and coordinate directly with the on-the-ground team.

What's the maximum group size?

Maximum 4 participants per group (small-group concept), keeping the trekking experience personal, safe, and flexible.
