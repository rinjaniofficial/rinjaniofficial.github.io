Sharing group (join group) prices per person, in USD. You trek together with other travelers — maximum 8 pax per group — on set departure routes: Sembalun, Senaru and Torean.

Read This First: What Your Price Covers

All Rinjani Official packages are sharing group (join group) treks — you join other travelers on the same route and date, with a maximum of 8 pax per group. Choose the price table that matches how you plan to arrive in Senaru or Sembalun:

Option 1: Trek Only — from USD 119/pax

The lowest price. Pre-trek pick-up and hotel are not included — you make your own way to the Senaru or Sembalun starting point and book your own stay the night before. After the trek we still take you back for free: a shared transfer to Bangsal Harbor, Senggigi or Mataram is included in the price.

Option 2: Full Package (Pick-up + Pre-Trek Hotel)

A complete, hassle-free package. Includes hotel pick-up in Lombok (Airport, Mataram, Senggigi, Bangsal Harbor), 1 night in Senaru before the trek, and drop-off after the trek. Priced per person by group size.

Prices below are for the trek itself and do not include pick-up from your hotel or airport, or a hotel before the trek. You meet our guide at the starting point in Senaru or Sembalun.
