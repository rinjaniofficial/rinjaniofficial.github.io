Choose the Package Type That Fits Your Travel Plan

We offer two flexible pricing structures for our Sharing / Join Group packages so you only pay for what you actually need:

Option 1: Trekking Only (No Pickup & No Pre-Hotel)

Ideal if you already have your own transport to Senaru or Sembalun and have booked your own accommodation prior to the trek. You meet our team directly at the start of the trek in Senaru or Sembalun.

Option 2: Full Package (Includes Pickup & Pre-Trek Hotel)

A complete hassle-free package. Includes hotel pick-up anywhere in Lombok (Airport, Mataram, Senggigi, Bangsal Harbor), 1-night accommodation in Senaru before the trek, and drop-off after the trek.

Option 1 Includes

  • Official Mount Rinjani National Park (TNGR) entrance ticket
  • Licensed guide & experienced porter team
  • Sharing Group concept, maximum 8 pax per group
  • 3 meals a day, fresh fruit, & drinking water throughout the trek
  • Camping gear: dome tent, sleeping bag, sleeping mat, air pillow, & portable toilet tent
  • Post-trek drop-off to the Bangsal, Senggigi, or Mataram area

Option 1 Excludes

  • Pre-trek pickup transport (self-arrival to the meeting point)
  • Accommodation the night before the trek
  • Travel insurance (required — covering trekking/hiking activities)
  • Personal outdoor gear (trekking shoes, warm jacket, headlamp, poles)
  • Tipping for guide and porter team (optional)
  • Flights or speedboat tickets to/from Lombok Island
